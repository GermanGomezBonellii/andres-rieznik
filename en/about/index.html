Profile

Physicist, neuroscientist and science communicator.

Andrés Rieznik holds a Bachelor's, Master's and PhD in Physics from Universidade Estadual de Campinas (Brazil). He is a full-time professor and researcher at the Neuroscience Laboratory of Universidad Torcuato Di Tella, where he works on topics related to neuroscience, human behavior and learning.

He combines research and teaching with an extensive track record in science communication. He is currently a science columnist for Nadie Dice Nada (Luzu TV) and Infobae. Between 2016 and 2022 he hosted La Liga de la Ciencia on Televisión Pública Argentina, a program recognized in 2019 with the Martín Fierro Award for Best Cultural Program and the FundTV Gold Award.

In recent years his work has focused especially on the neuroscience of learning and literacy. He is part of the Kalulu team, an evidence-based literacy project, and is the author of several popular-science books on neuroscience, cognition and education, including Neuromagia, Atletismo mental, Retos asombrosos, Tabú and Enseñar.
